Daily Hub privacy policy

Daily Hub is a personal app. It has no account, no server and no ads.

What is stored

Everything you enter (tasks, notes, meals, workouts, spends, habits, smoking log and settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone.

Health data

If you connect Health Connect, Daily Hub reads your steps, distance and active calories. It only reads this data, to show it to you inside the app. It never writes to Health Connect and never sends health data anywhere.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to fetch the weather for the city you choose (Open-Meteo) and to load exercise demonstration pictures (jsDelivr). Only the city's coordinates and the picture address are sent. Nothing about you is sent.

Backups

A backup file is created only when you tap "Save backup". You choose where it goes, for example Google Drive. Restoring reads a file you pick.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything.
